TELEVISION ENERGY

How Much Energy Does a TV Use?

Television energy consumption depends on factors such as screen size, display technology, brightness and how long the television is used each day.

UNDERSTANDING TV POWER

What affects television energy consumption?

Different televisions can consume different amounts of electricity. Larger screens and certain display technologies may require more power, while newer energy-efficient models can reduce electricity use.

Screen Size

Larger televisions generally require more energy than smaller screens because they have larger display panels.

Display Technology

Different display technologies can have different energy requirements depending on how the screen produces its image.

Usage Time

A television that operates for several hours every day will consume more electricity than one that is used occasionally.

ENERGY SAVING

Tips for reducing TV energy use

Turn It Off

Turn the television off when nobody is watching rather than leaving it running in the background.

Adjust Brightness

Reducing unnecessary screen brightness can help lower the amount of electricity consumed.

Use Energy Saving Modes

Many modern televisions include energy-saving settings that can reduce power consumption.
